Why Natural

If we can't picture it growing, it doesn't go in the bag.

Most “natural” snacks lean on flavor labs to fake a taste. We build ours from the real thing — whole nuts, real spice, actual fruit — even when it costs more and lasts less time on a shelf.

  • Whole-food protein. Nuts, seeds, and nut butters — never isolates or powders.
  • Short lists, on purpose. Most flavors use six ingredients or fewer.
  • Real spice, real flavor. Cardamom, rosewater, tahini — not lab-built extracts.
  • Built to hold up. Naturally binding ingredients, no gums or fillers.

The Name

Timpanogos, at first light.

Every clear morning in the Wasatch Range, the sun catches Mount Timpanogos before it reaches the valley floor — the ridgeline turning shades of plum and rose while the base stays shadowed stone. That's the color story behind timp: alpenglow purple, canyon clay, and the cream of first light on snow.
